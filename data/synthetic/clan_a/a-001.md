# Aldermoor Energy trading operations overview

**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Document type:** Overview

## Purpose of this document

This overview describes how Aldermoor Energy's trading operations are organised, which markets we trade in, and the systems and units we use. It is intended as a general reference for staff across the business. It does not replace desk procedures, risk limits or policy documents, which remain the authoritative sources for those matters.

## Company and markets

Aldermoor Energy is a UK gas and power utility with a retail and industrial supply book. Our trading activity supports that book by managing the price and volume exposure that comes with supplying gas and electricity to customers.

We trade gas at Kestrel Hub. We trade power in the GB market. Both activities feed into a single view of the company's position, so desks should record all trades consistently in the central system.

## Front office structure

The front office is organised into three desks:

- **Gas desk:** trades gas at Kestrel Hub and manages gas exposure from the supply book.
- **Power desk:** trades power in the GB market and manages power exposure.
- **Retail hedging desk:** hedges the volume and price risk arising from retail and industrial supply commitments.

Desks work closely together. Where a trade or hedge affects another desk's exposure, the affected desk should be informed through the normal internal channels.

## Units of measure

Units are fixed and must be used consistently in all trade records, confirmations and internal reports:

- **Gas** is traded in **kWh**. This is the official gas trading unit.
- **Power** is traded in **MWh**.

Do not convert between units informally in trade records. If a report needs a different presentation, state the conversion clearly.

## Trading platform

All trades are recorded in **Lumen ETRM**, our trading and risk platform. It is hosted on premises in the **Leeds data centre**. Staff should capture trades in Lumen ETRM at the point of execution and should not keep parallel records outside the system for the same deal.

Questions about platform access, data quality or system behaviour should be raised through the appropriate internal support route rather than resolved locally.
