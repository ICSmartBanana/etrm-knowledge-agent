# Gas Day-Ahead Nomination Procedure

**Aldermoor Energy: Internal Procedure**
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure sets out how Aldermoor Energy's gas trading and scheduling staff submit day-ahead nominations for the retail and industrial supply book. It applies to all nominations made for trading at the Kestrel Hub. Staff should read it alongside the Lumen ETRM user guide, which covers system access and position checks.

## Key Definitions

- **Gas day:** The gas day runs from 06:00 to 06:00 UK local time. All volumes, positions and nominations are assigned to the gas day in which they flow, not to the calendar date on which they are entered.
- **D-1:** The calendar day before the gas day to which a nomination applies.
- **Nomination quantity:** Nominations are expressed in kWh per gas day. Staff must not enter quantities in other units, and must not convert between units without checking the conversion in Lumen ETRM.

## Submission Process

1. Confirm the expected daily demand and supply positions for each portfolio segment, using the latest forecast and the open position in Lumen ETRM.
2. Enter the nomination quantities in kWh per gas day against the correct Kestrel Hub entry and exit points.
3. Check that the total nominated supply matches the total nominated demand before submitting.
4. Submit the nominations from Lumen ETRM through the NomBridge interface.
5. Record the submission time and confirmation reference in the trade log.

## Deadlines

Day-ahead nominations must reach the Kestrel Hub scheduling system by 13:00 UK time on D-1. Staff should aim to submit well before this cut-off, as the system does not accept nominations that arrive after the deadline unless they have been approved as late nominations.

## Late Nominations

A late nomination is any nomination that misses the 13:00 UK time on D-1 deadline. Late nominations require approval from the Head of Gas Scheduling before submission. The requester must provide the reason for the delay, the quantities involved and the affected gas day. Staff must not submit a late nomination until approval has been recorded in the trade log.
