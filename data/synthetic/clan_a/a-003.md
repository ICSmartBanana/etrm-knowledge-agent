# Gas Within-Day Renomination Rules

**Document type:** Procedure
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Scope:** Gas trading and scheduling staff, and anyone supporting transport nominations for Aldermoor Energy's retail and industrial supply book.

## Purpose

This procedure sets out when and how within-day renominations may be submitted for gas traded at the Kestrel Hub, and the controls that apply once a renomination is accepted. It applies to all transport contracts held by Aldermoor Energy.

## Acceptance Cut-Off

Within-day renominations are accepted until 03:00 UK time on the gas day. Requests received after this time must not be submitted as within-day renominations. Staff who identify a late requirement should escalate it through the normal shift handover so that the position can be managed under the appropriate route.

## Timing of Effect

A renomination takes effect no earlier than two full hours after it is accepted. Traders must allow for this lag when planning flow changes, and must not assume that a renomination accepted late in the day will flow immediately. Any change in position that depends on an earlier effective time must be agreed before the renomination is submitted.

## Frequency Limit

Aldermoor allows at most 24 renominations per gas day per transport contract. The count applies to each transport contract separately. Staff should record each submission in the position log so that the running total is visible to the desk at all times. Where a contract is approaching the limit, the desk lead must be informed before any further submission is made.

## Capacity Check for North Basin Transmission

Renominations on the North Basin Transmission route need a capacity check first. The check must be completed and recorded before the renomination is submitted. A renomination on this route without a completed check must not be submitted, even if it falls within the acceptance window and the frequency limit.

## Recording and Systems

All renominations must be entered in Lumen ETRM with the transport contract reference, the requested change, the time of acceptance, and the effective time. Entries must be made at the time of submission and not backdated. Any discrepancy between the platform record and the transport confirmation should be raised immediately with the scheduling team.

## Review

This procedure is reviewed when transport arrangements change or when the frequency limit or acceptance cut-off is revised. Questions about interpretation should be directed to the gas scheduling lead before the relevant gas day begins.
