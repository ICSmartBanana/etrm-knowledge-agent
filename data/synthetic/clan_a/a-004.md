# Lumen ETRM gas scheduling configuration

## Purpose and scope

This document describes how the gas scheduling module is configured in Lumen ETRM at Aldermoor Energy. It is intended for staff in gas trading, scheduling, and risk who need to understand how daily nominations are generated and checked. Lumen ETRM is an Allegro-style trading and risk platform hosted on premises. Aldermoor Energy trades gas at the Kestrel Hub and power in the GB market, and the gas scheduling configuration supports the retail and industrial supply book.

This document takes effect on 2025-10-01.

## System version

The scheduling module runs in Lumen ETRM version 7.4.2. Any change to scheduling logic, batch timing, or tolerance settings should be recorded against this version. If the platform is upgraded, this document must be reviewed before the new version is used for nominations.

## Nomination batch job

Daily nominations are produced by the batch job named GS_NOM_DAILY. The job runs at 11:30 UK time. Scheduling staff should not assume that a nomination exists for a contract until the batch has completed. If the job does not complete as expected, the scheduling team should follow the internal incident process and inform the gas trading desk before the nomination window closes.

Staff should check the job status in the platform's batch monitor after the scheduled run. Any manual rerun should be logged with a short reason, so that the audit trail shows why the nominations were regenerated.

## Nomination tolerance

A nomination is flagged for review when its quantity differs from the contracted quantity by more than the tolerance. The tolerance is 2 percent of the contracted quantity. Flagged nominations are held in the exceptions queue for manual review. Reviewers should confirm the reason for the difference, such as a customer-notified change in offtake, before releasing the nomination.

The tolerance is a configuration setting, not a per-contract override. Requests to change it must go through the change control process and be approved by gas risk.

## Pipeline route codes

Pipeline route codes are prefixed NBT- for North Basin Transmission. When entering or reviewing route codes in a nomination, staff should confirm that the code begins with NBT- before selecting the route for a North Basin Transmission entry or exit point. Route codes that do not follow this prefix convention should be queried with the scheduling team rather than edited directly.

## Responsibilities and change control

Changes to the scheduling configuration are controlled through the standard change process. Each change should state the affected version, the batch job, and the tolerance or route settings involved. The gas risk function reviews any change that affects tolerances, and the scheduling team confirms that the batch timing still fits the nomination deadlines before the change is released.

## Related guidance

Staff should read this document alongside the Lumen ETRM user guide for the gas scheduling screens and the internal incident process for batch failures. Questions about this configuration should be raised with the gas scheduling team through the usual internal channels.
