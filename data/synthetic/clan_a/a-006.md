# GB Power Scheduling and Notification Procedure

**Company:** Aldermoor Energy
**Document type:** Procedure
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure sets out how Aldermoor Energy notifies its power positions in the GB market and how the power scheduling process is run within Lumen ETRM, the company's on-premises trading and risk platform. It applies to all staff involved in power trading, scheduling, and position management. Gas trading at the Kestrel Hub is covered by separate procedures and is outside the scope of this document.

## Notification Principles

Aldermoor Energy notifies power positions per **half-hourly settlement period**. Each settlement period's position must be accurate, complete, and reflect all trades that affect physical delivery for that period. Positions should be reviewed before notification to confirm that volumes, delivery periods, and asset references match the trade records in Lumen ETRM.

## Final Physical Notifications

Final physical notifications (FPNs) are sent **one hour** before gate closure for each settlement period. Traders must confirm their final position for each affected period ahead of that deadline. Any change made after the FPN has been sent must follow the amendment process and be recorded with a reason, so that the audit trail remains complete.

## Scheduling Job

The power scheduling job in Lumen ETRM is **PS_FPN_HH**. The job collates the approved power positions for each settlement period and prepares the notification data. Staff must not run, edit, or reschedule PS_FPN_HH without authorisation from the power trading desk lead. If the job fails or produces unexpected output, the operator must stop further notifications for the affected period and escalate immediately.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Traders are responsible for the accuracy of their positions and for confirming them before the notification deadline. Schedulers are responsible for monitoring the PS_FPN_HH job and checking its outputs against trader confirmations. Risk and middle office staff review exceptions and maintain records.

All notifications, confirmations, job outputs, and amendments must be retained in line with company records policy. Records should allow any notified position to be traced back to the trade data and approvals behind it.
