# Gas and Power Invoicing and Settlement Summary

**Aldermoor Energy: Internal Procedure**
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01

## Purpose

This procedure summarises how Aldermoor Energy issues invoices and settles payments for gas and power supplied through our retail and industrial supply book. It applies to staff in Settlements, Trading Operations, and Customer Accounts who prepare, review, or query invoices. It covers gas traded at the Kestrel Hub and power traded in the GB market.

## Invoice Issue

Gas and power invoices are issued on the 10th calendar day of the following month. Each invoice covers the volumes and values recorded for the preceding delivery month. Before issue, Settlements checks that the volumes and prices in Lumen ETRM agree to the confirmed trade records and the associated metering or nomination data.

Staff should not issue ad hoc invoices outside this cycle unless the Settlements manager has approved the exception in writing.

## Currency and Payment Terms

All invoices are in GBP. Where a contract references a different currency, Settlements converts the amount using the rate defined in the contract terms and states the conversion basis on the invoice.

Payment terms are net 15 days from the invoice date. Customer Accounts tracks due dates and flags overdue balances to the account owner.

## Queries and Disputes

Customers and internal teams may raise queries about an invoice before the due date. Disputed amounts should be logged with the invoice reference, the nature of the query, and supporting evidence. Settlements reviews the query against Lumen ETRM records and confirms whether the invoice should be reissued, credited, or upheld. Undisputed portions of an invoice remain payable on the normal terms.
