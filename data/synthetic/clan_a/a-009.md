# Imbalance Charge Policy (Version 1)

**Company:** Aldermoor Energy
**Document type:** Policy
**Version:** 1
**Effective date:** 2023-01-01

*This is an archived version. It describes the requirements that applied from the effective date above.*

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Aldermoor Energy identifies, escalates and reports imbalance exposure arising from its gas and power trading activity. It applies to the retail and industrial supply book and to all trading positions held in the GB market and at the Kestrel Hub.

Imbalance charges can accumulate quickly when physical nominations diverge from contracted volumes. Early reporting allows the risk desk to review positions and decide on corrective action before charges crystallise.

## Reporting Threshold

Imbalance exposure above **500,000 kWh** per gas day must be reported to the risk desk. The threshold applies to the net exposure for the gas day, after allowable netting across the supply portfolio.

Staff should not wait for end-of-day settlement figures before escalating. If a forecast indicates that exposure will exceed the threshold, the position owner should treat it as reportable.

## Reporting Deadline

Reports are due by **10:00** the next working day. Reports submitted after this time are treated as late and must be flagged in the exception log with a short explanation.

Where a report covers more than one gas day, each gas day must be listed separately so that the risk desk can reconcile exposure against the relevant trade records.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the net imbalance exposure for the gas day using the figures held in Lumen ETRM.
2. Compare the exposure against the 500,000 kWh threshold.
3. If the threshold is exceeded, prepare the report with the gas day, the net volume, the contributing portfolios and any known corrective actions.
4. Submit the report to the risk desk by 10:00 the next working day.
5. Record the submission in the imbalance log, noting the time of submission.

Position owners remain responsible for the accuracy of data entered into Lumen ETRM. Errors identified after submission should be reported to the risk desk as soon as they are found, with a corrected figure.

## Records

Copies of all imbalance reports, exception log entries and supporting calculations must be retained in line with Aldermoor Energy's standard records retention practice. Records should be sufficient to show when exposure was identified, when it was reported and what action followed.
