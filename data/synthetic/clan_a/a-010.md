# Imbalance Charge Policy (Version 2)

**Document type:** Policy
**Company:** Aldermoor Energy
**Effective date:** 2025-04-01
**Status:** Version 2. This version replaces version 1 from April 2025.

## Purpose

This policy sets out how Aldermoor Energy staff identify, record and report imbalance exposure arising from gas and power trading and from the retail and industrial supply book. It applies to all teams that hold or manage positions that could contribute to imbalance, including trading, supply operations and settlement.

Staff may quote this policy in internal correspondence. Where a practice described here conflicts with an earlier instruction, this version takes precedence from its effective date.

## Reporting threshold

Imbalance exposure above **350,000 kWh** per gas day must be reported to the risk desk. The threshold applies to the net exposure for each gas day, not to individual deals or meter points. Staff should net offsetting positions within the same gas day before comparing the result with the threshold.

Exposure should be calculated from the position data held in Lumen ETRM, the trading and risk platform hosted on premises. If the figure in Lumen ETRM appears inconsistent with supply operations data, the discrepancy should be noted in the report rather than resolved informally.

## Reporting deadline

Reports are due by **09:00** the next working day after the gas day to which the exposure relates. A report submitted after this time is treated as late and should include a short explanation of the delay.

Reports should state:

- the gas day concerned;
- the net imbalance exposure in kWh;
- the main contributing positions or supply portfolios;
- any actions already taken to reduce the exposure.

## Roles and responsibilities

Trading and supply operations teams are responsible for identifying exposure that crosses the threshold and for preparing the report. The risk desk reviews each report, confirms the figures against the platform, and decides whether further action is needed.

Staff who are unsure whether an exposure requires reporting should err on the side of reporting it and seek guidance from the risk desk.

## Transition from version 1

Version 1 of this policy applied until March 2025. From April 2025, version 2 governs all imbalance reporting. Teams should review any local procedures that refer to version 1 and update them to match this document.
