# Counterparty credit observations (internal)

**Internal - not for distribution**

**Company:** Aldermoor Energy
**Document type:** Analysis
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01

## Purpose

This analysis records the internal credit view of Fenwick Gas Supply, a counterparty to Aldermoor Energy's gas trading activity. It summarises recent payment behaviour and sets out the proposed response from the credit risk function. It is an internal assessment only.

## Background

Aldermoor Energy is a UK gas and power utility with a retail and industrial supply book. It trades gas at the Kestrel Hub and power in the GB market. Trade capture, settlement and risk positions are recorded in Lumen ETRM, which is hosted on premises. Counterparty exposure is reviewed against approved limits as part of routine credit monitoring.

## Observations

Fenwick Gas Supply has recorded three late payments in 2025. Each late payment has been reviewed against the invoice history and the agreed settlement terms. Late settlement, even where the amount is eventually paid, increases the period during which Aldermoor Energy carries unsecured exposure to the counterparty.

Taken together, the pattern indicates weakening payment discipline. The observations do not, on their own, establish a default risk, but they are sufficient to justify a more conservative limit while the position is monitored.

## Proposed action

The credit risk function proposes reducing the unsecured limit for Fenwick Gas Supply to GBP 2 million. The reduction would apply to new exposure and would be reviewed against payment performance over the following period. Trading desks should be notified of the revised limit before it takes effect so that new trades can be checked against it in Lumen ETRM.

## Handling

This assessment is not shared with the counterparty. Discussion of the limit change with Fenwick Gas Supply should be limited to the standard notification process approved by credit risk. Staff should not reference this analysis, or the reasons for it, in external correspondence.

## Next steps

- Credit risk to confirm the revised limit and its effective date.
- Trading and settlement teams to be informed so that exposure checks reflect the new limit.
- Payment performance to be reviewed at the next scheduled credit committee.
