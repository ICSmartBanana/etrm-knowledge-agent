# Lumen ETRM end-of-day batch runbook (wiki copy)

_Copied from the operations wiki; may be out of date._

**Company:** Aldermoor Energy
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Applies to:** Lumen ETRM, the trading and risk platform hosted on premises at Aldermoor Energy

## Purpose

This runbook describes how the Lumen ETRM end-of-day (EOD) batch is run each trading day. The batch values gas positions traded at the Kestrel Hub and power positions in the GB market, then produces the risk reports used by the wider business. Staff may quote this document when confirming batch procedure.

## Batch Schedule

The end-of-day batch starts at **19:00 UK time**. It runs its steps in a fixed order:

1. Price import
2. **EOD_MTM** (end-of-day mark-to-market valuation)
3. Risk reports

Each step must complete before the next one begins. Do not start a later step manually while an earlier step is still running, because the risk reports depend on valuations produced by EOD_MTM.

## Price Import Failure

If the price import fails, do not proceed to EOD_MTM. Instead:

1. Check the batch log to identify the cause of the failure.
2. Confirm that the source price files are present and complete.
3. Rerun job **PX_IMPORT_RETRY** before 20:00.
4. Once PX_IMPORT_RETRY completes successfully, allow the batch to continue with EOD_MTM and the risk reports.

If PX_IMPORT_RETRY does not complete before 20:00, escalate to the batch support rota immediately, as the remaining steps will be compressed.

## Risk Report Publication

Risk reports must be published by **21:30 UK time**. Before publishing, confirm that:

- EOD_MTM has completed without errors.
- The report totals reconcile with the valuation outputs.
- Any exceptions noted in the batch log have been recorded.

Publication after 21:30 UK time must be reported to the risk function, with the reason for the delay and the expected publication time.
