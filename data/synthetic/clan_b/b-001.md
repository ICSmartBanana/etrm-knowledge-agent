# Brightwater Refined Products trading operations overview

**Document type:** Overview
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01

## Purpose

This overview describes how Brightwater Refined Products runs its trading and supply activities. Staff may quote it when briefing colleagues, clients, or new team members. It gives a high-level picture of what we trade, where we store product, which systems we use, and how gas supply supports our operations.

## Business activities

Brightwater is a refined products trader. We trade diesel, jet fuel and gasoline, and we operate two storage terminals that hold product for our trading book and for scheduled deliveries. Our trading desk manages positions across these products, monitors storage levels at both terminals, and coordinates physical movements with operations staff.

Refined products are traded in metric tonnes. All contract quantities, position reports, and inventory figures should be stated in metric tonnes unless a specific document says otherwise.

## Gas supply and the Marrick blending site

Brightwater buys gas at Kestrel Hub. This purchasing has one purpose: to fuel the Marrick blending site. We do not buy gas at Kestrel Hub for trading or resale.

Gas is scheduled in therms. Nominations, confirmations, and allocation records for gas should use therms as the unit of measure, so that they reconcile with the supply side of the Marrick blending site's operations.

## Trading platform

Brightwater runs Corvane CTRM for trade capture, position management, and settlement support. Corvane CTRM is hosted as software as a service (SaaS), so the platform is maintained by its host rather than on Brightwater's own servers. Staff who need platform access or who spot a discrepancy in trade records should raise it through the normal internal channels for their team.

## Working practices

- Record every refined products trade in Corvane CTRM with its quantity in metric tonnes.
- Schedule gas for the Marrick blending site in therms, and keep those schedules separate from refined products positions.
- Reconcile terminal inventory against trade records regularly, at both storage terminals.
- Do not describe gas purchases at Kestrel Hub as trading activity. They exist to supply the Marrick blending site.

Questions about this overview should go to the trading operations lead for your team.
