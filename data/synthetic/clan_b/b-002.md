# Gas Nomination Procedure for the Marrick Blending Site

**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Document type:** Procedure
**Owner:** Gas and utilities scheduling

## Purpose

This procedure sets out how Brightwater Refined Products nominates gas supply for the Marrick blending site. The Marrick site buys gas at the Kestrel Hub to fuel blending operations. Staff who prepare, check, or chase nominations should follow these steps so that gas flows match the blending plan.

## Roles and Responsibilities

Brightwater does not nominate directly. A third-party **scheduling agent** submits nominations to the Kestrel Hub on Brightwater's behalf. Brightwater's responsibility is to provide accurate nomination volumes to the agent on time.

Day-to-day ownership sits with Lucas Ferreira-Hale, Gas and utilities scheduler, who is the first point of contact for questions about nomination content, timing, or exceptions.

## Nomination Timing

Day-ahead nominations must reach the scheduling agent by **14:30 UK time on D-1**. D-1 refers to the day before the gas day to which the nomination applies. Late nominations may not be accepted by the agent and could leave the Marrick site short of gas for blending.

## Nomination Format

Nominations are expressed in **therms per gas day**. Do not enter volumes in other units. Check the unit field before submission, and confirm that the daily quantity matches the blending schedule for the gas day concerned.

## Submission Method

Corvane CTRM generates the nomination and sends it to the scheduling agent as a **CSV file over SFTP**. Users must not edit the CSV file by hand after generation. If a correction is needed, amend the source data in Corvane CTRM and regenerate the file.

## Checks Before Submission

Before the file is sent, the scheduler should:

- Confirm the gas day and the nomination volume against the approved blending plan.
- Confirm the unit is therms per gas day.
- Confirm the file has been generated from the current source data.

After sending, confirm the transfer was completed. If there is no acknowledgement or the transfer appears to have failed, treat the nomination as at risk and escalate at once.

## Escalation and Contacts

If a nomination is at risk of missing the deadline, or if the agent reports a problem, contact the Scheduling agent hotline:

**Scheduling agent hotline:** +44 20 7946 0733

Then inform Lucas Ferreira-Hale, Gas and utilities scheduler, so that the blending team can assess the impact on Marrick site operations.

## Review

This procedure is reviewed when the scheduling agent arrangements, Corvane CTRM configuration, or Marrick blending plan change.
