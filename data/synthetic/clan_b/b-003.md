# Gas Within-Day Changes for the Marrick Site

**Document type:** Procedure
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Applies to:** Gas nominations for the Marrick blending site

## Purpose

This procedure sets out how the gas desk at Brightwater Refined Products submits within-day changes to the gas supply schedule for the Marrick site. Brightwater buys gas at the Kestrel Hub to fuel the Marrick blending site. Staff should follow these steps so that changes reach the scheduling agent on time and within the daily limit.

## Deadline and Limits

- Within-day changes must reach the scheduling agent by **01:00 UK time** on the gas day.
- Brightwater makes at most **four within-day changes** per gas day. Plan adjustments so that the desk does not need a fifth change.
- Each change incurs a fixed agent fee of **GBP 150**. Include this cost when assessing whether a change is worth making.

Changes that arrive after 01:00 UK time should not be sent without approval from the desk lead, because late submissions may not be accepted by the scheduling agent.

## Steps

1. **Review the position.** Check the Marrick site's gas requirement against the current nominations in Corvane CTRM, Brightwater's commodity trading platform.
2. **Confirm the need for a change.** Compare the expected blending demand with the nominated volume. Count the changes already made on the gas day before proceeding.
3. **Estimate the cost.** Add the GBP 150 agent fee to the expected cost or benefit of the change. Record the estimate in the trade notes.
4. **Prepare the change.** Enter the revised quantity in Corvane CTRM and check it against the Kestrel Hub capacity position.
5. **Submit before the deadline.** Send the change to the scheduling agent well before 01:00 UK time, and keep the confirmation with the trade record.
6. **Log the change.** Note the change number for the gas day (out of the four permitted) and the reason for the change.

## Controls and Records

- Each within-day change must have a recorded reason and a matching confirmation from the scheduling agent.
- The desk lead reviews the change log at the end of each gas day.
- Any request to exceed four within-day changes on a gas day must be escalated to the desk lead before submission.

## Review

This procedure takes effect on 2025-10-01. Staff who have questions about the deadline, the change limit or the agent fee should raise them with the desk lead before the gas day begins.
