# Corvane CTRM Settlement and Invoicing Configuration

## Purpose and Scope

This document describes how settlement and invoicing are configured in Corvane CTRM for Brightwater Refined Products. It covers refined products deals, including diesel, jet fuel, and gasoline, across the company's storage terminals. Staff may quote this document when questions arise about invoice timing, payment terms, or settlement currency. The configuration took effect on 2025-10-01.

Brightwater buys gas at the Kestrel Hub to fuel its blending site. Corvane CTRM is the commodity trading platform that Brightwater uses for these activities, and it is hosted as SaaS.

## Invoicing Schedule

Sales invoices are generated on the 5th business day of the following month. Invoice generation is carried out by the Corvane CTRM invoicing job named INV_MONTHLY_RUN. Operations and finance staff should not expect invoices for a given month's deals to be produced before this run completes, and should plan month-end cut-offs with this timing in mind.

Business-day counting follows the company's standard working calendar. Any changes to the calendar should be reflected in the job schedule by the system administrator before the start of the affected month.

## Payment Terms

Standard payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date. Customers receive the invoice generated by INV_MONTHLY_RUN and have 30 days from that invoice date to remit payment. Collections staff should track due dates from the invoice date recorded in Corvane CTRM rather than from the end of the deal month.

## Settlement Currency

The settlement currency is USD for all refined products deals. Trade capture should default to USD for diesel, jet fuel, and gasoline transactions. Where a counterparty requests another currency, the request must be escalated before the deal is booked, since the standard configuration does not support non-USD settlement for these products.

## Operational Notes

- Confirm that INV_MONTHLY_RUN has completed before issuing any monthly invoice summaries.
- Use net 30 days from the invoice date when setting customer due dates in receivables reporting.
- Verify that all refined products deals carry USD as the settlement currency before month-end close.
- Report any failed or delayed run of INV_MONTHLY_RUN to the Corvane CTRM support contact listed by the finance systems team.

## Review

This configuration should be reviewed at least once per year, or whenever commercial terms change. Changes require approval from the finance systems team before they are applied in Corvane CTRM.
