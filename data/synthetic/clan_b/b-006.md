## Purpose

This runbook sets out how operations staff at Brightwater Refined Products handle vessel and barge cargo timing, notice of readiness, and demurrage claims. It applies to diesel, jet fuel, and gasoline movements through Brightwater's storage terminals, and to gas purchases made at the Kestrel Hub for the company's blending site. Staff may quote this document in internal discussions and handover notes.

## Notice of Readiness and Laytime

Laytime is the permitted time for discharge or loading under the charter terms. Under this runbook, laytime starts **six hours** after notice of readiness is tendered. Operations staff should record the exact tender time in the cargo file as soon as it is issued, because the laytime start is calculated from that timestamp and not from when the vessel or barge reached berth.

Before tendering notice of readiness, the operations desk should confirm:

- The receiving tank or terminal line is clear and available.
- Required documentation, including bills of lading and quality certificates, is ready for the terminal.
- The cargo quantity figures match the nomination.

If readiness conditions change after tender, log the change in the cargo file and inform the commercial desk the same day.

## Recording Demurrage

Demurrage is recorded in Corvane CTRM under cost type **DEM-CLAIM**. Enter the amount against the relevant cargo deal so that it appears in the position and settlement reports. Do not record demurrage under general freight or terminal fee cost types, as this distorts the reporting used by the commercial desk.

When a demurrage amount is calculated, attach the supporting time sheet, the notice of readiness record, and the discharge completion record to the Corvane CTRM entry.

## Filing Demurrage Claims

Demurrage claims must be filed within **60 days** of discharge. The clock runs from the completion of discharge recorded in the cargo file. Claims filed after this window may be rejected by the counterparty, and any resulting loss is the responsibility of the desk that failed to file in time.

Recommended steps:

1. Confirm the discharge completion date and the laytime calculation.
2. Record the DEM-CLAIM entry in Corvane CTRM.
3. Prepare the claim package with all supporting documents.
4. Submit the claim to the counterparty and note the submission date in the cargo file.
5. Diarise a follow-up check at 30 days if no response has been received.

## Escalation

Disputes over laytime start times or calculated amounts should be raised with the commercial desk lead before any settlement is agreed. Operations staff should not accept or reject a counterparty's figures without a documented review.
