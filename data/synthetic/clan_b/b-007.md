# Corvane CTRM Daily Position and P&L Runbook

**Company:** Brightwater Refined Products
**Document type:** Runbook
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01

## Purpose

This runbook describes how the trading operations team runs the daily position freeze and end-of-day profit and loss (P&L) process in Corvane CTRM. Brightwater trades diesel, jet fuel, and gasoline and operates storage terminals. The company also buys gas at the Kestrel Hub to supply its blending site. Corvane CTRM, a commodity trading platform hosted as SaaS, is the system of record for these positions and for P&L.

Staff may quote this document when explaining the daily cycle to colleagues in other teams.

## Daily Position Freeze

Positions are frozen at **17:00 Houston time** each trading day. After the freeze:

- Trade captures entered after the cutoff roll into the next business day's position.
- Any correction to a frozen position must be logged with a reason and approved by the senior diesel trader before it is reflected in the P&L run.
- Operations staff should confirm the freeze status in Corvane CTRM before starting the P&L process.

## End-of-Day P&L Process

The P&L job is **PNL_EOD_CORE**. It must finish before **19:30 Houston time**. Operations staff should:

- Confirm that the position freeze completed without errors.
- Start PNL_EOD_CORE once the freeze is confirmed.
- Monitor the job until it reports completion.
- Escalate to the senior diesel trader if the job has not finished by the deadline.

## Late Market Data

If market data is late, the process still continues. In that case:

- Use the previous day's settlement price for the affected instruments.
- Flag the run in Corvane CTRM so that the P&L is marked as based on provisional prices.
- Record the affected instruments and the reason for the delay in the run notes.
- Revisit the flagged run once current market data is available, and rerun the affected valuations as required.
