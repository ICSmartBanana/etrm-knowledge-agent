# Product Quality Claims Policy (Version 1)

**Company:** Brightwater Refined Products
**Document type:** Policy
**Effective date:** 2022-06-01
**Status:** Superseded (retained for reference)

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Brightwater Refined Products handles quality claims on refined products it receives or delivers, including diesel, jet fuel, and gasoline. It applies to product moved through the company's storage terminals and to cargoes supplied to the blending site that draws gas from the Kestrel Hub.

## 2. Scope

The policy covers all staff in trading, terminal operations, and blending who are involved in receiving, delivering, or certifying product quality. It applies whether a claim concerns a purchase, a sale, or a transfer between company locations.

## 3. Lodging a Quality Claim

Quality claims must be lodged within 14 days of the discharge date. The discharge date is the date on which the product was completed discharged at the receiving point, as recorded in the terminal log.

A claim should include:

- the cargo or batch reference
- the product grade and specification being disputed
- the results of any sampling or testing carried out
- supporting certificates of quality and bills of lading
- a summary of the expected commercial impact

Claims lodged after the 14-day window will generally not be accepted unless the counterparty agrees in writing to waive the deadline.

## 4. Sampling and Testing

Where practical, samples should be drawn at the point of discharge under the agreed sampling procedure and retained in sealed containers. Testing should be carried out against the specification in the relevant contract. Staff should record the time, location, and method of each sample.

## 5. Recording in the Trading System

All claims must be logged in the Corvane CTRM platform against the relevant deal, with attachments uploaded and the claim status kept current. Trading staff are responsible for updating the status when the counterparty responds.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

Terminal operations staff confirm discharge dates and retain samples. Trading staff prepare and lodge claims. Blending staff notify trading promptly if product received for blending appears to be off specification.

This policy was reviewed as part of the annual document cycle. It has been superseded by a later version and should not be used for current claims.
