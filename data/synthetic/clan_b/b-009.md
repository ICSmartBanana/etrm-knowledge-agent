# Product Quality Claims Policy (Version 2)

**Company:** Brightwater Refined Products
**Document type:** Policy
**Effective date:** 2025-01-15
**Applies to:** Diesel, jet fuel and gasoline purchased or received into Brightwater storage terminals

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Brightwater Refined Products raises, supports and tracks claims about the quality of refined products delivered to its storage terminals or to its blending site. Consistent handling protects the company's margins and its commercial relationships with suppliers and counterparties.

## 2. Scope

The policy covers all quality disputes arising from cargoes and parcels of diesel, jet fuel and gasoline. It applies to trading, operations, terminal and blending staff who identify or handle a potential off-specification delivery. Gas purchased at the Kestrel Hub for the blending site is within scope where a quality issue is identified at the point of receipt.

## 3. Claim Deadline

Quality claims must be lodged within 21 days of the discharge date. The discharge date is the date on which the product was fully discharged into Brightwater's tanks, as recorded in the terminal log. Claims received after this window may be rejected by the counterparty, and the company may lose its right to recover losses. Staff who suspect a problem must escalate it immediately so that the deadline is not missed.

## 4. Independent Inspection Requirement

Every quality claim must be supported by an independent inspector's report. Brightwater staff must not rely solely on internal test results when submitting a claim. The inspector should be appointed promptly after discharge, and the report must identify the sampling method, the test results against the contract specification, and the tank or line from which samples were drawn.

## 5. Procedure

1. **Identify:** Terminal or blending staff flag any sample, test or observation outside specification and record it in the terminal log.
2. **Notify:** The trading desk and the quality team are informed the same working day.
3. **Appoint:** Quality control arranges an independent inspector and secures retained samples.
4. **Record:** All claim activity is logged in Corvane CTRM, the company's commodity trading platform, so that the discharge date and deadline are tracked against each deal.
5. **Lodge:** The claim is submitted to the counterparty with the inspector's report attached, within the 21-day window.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

Trading is accountable for lodging the claim on time. Terminal operations are responsible for accurate discharge records and sample retention. Quality control owns the inspector appointment and the technical content of the report. The commercial manager approves any decision to withdraw or settle a claim.

## 7. Review

This policy is version 2 and takes effect on 2025-01-15. It supersedes version 1. Questions about interpretation should be raised with the quality team before any deadline is at risk.
