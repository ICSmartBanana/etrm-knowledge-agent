# Settlement Terms Quick Reference (Old Wiki Page)

**Document type:** Wiki page (informal, legacy)

**Effective date:** 2023-09-10
**Status:** Archived for reference. Staff may quote it, but check current terms before relying on it.

## Purpose

This page gives a short summary of the settlement terms that Brightwater Refined Products has used for physical and financial refined product trades. It covers diesel, jet fuel, and gasoline sold from our storage terminals, and gas bought at the Kestrel Hub to supply our blending site. The page is informal and was written for quick reference, not as a controlled procedure.

## Standard Payment Terms

The standard payment term noted on this page is **net 20 days**. Invoices issued under standard terms are due for payment within that window from the invoice date. Counterparties with negotiated contracts may have different terms, and those contract terms take precedence over this page.

## Settlement Workflow

Settlement generally follows these steps:

1. Confirm the trade details, including product, volume, price, and delivery window, against the signed confirmation.
2. Check that the invoice matches the confirmation before it is released.
3. Record the invoice in the trading system so the due date is tracked.
4. Follow up on open items as the due date approaches, and escalate any disputes to the trade support team.
5. Post the receipt and close the item once payment clears.

## System Notes
