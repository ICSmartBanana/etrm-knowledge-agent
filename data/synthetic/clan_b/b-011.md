# Supplier Margin Analysis (Internal)

**Internal - not for distribution**

**Document type:** Analysis
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Prepared for:** Brightwater Refined Products internal use only

## Purpose

This analysis reviews the pricing position of Ostrava Petrochem, one of Brightwater's refined products suppliers, and sets out how its terms bear on our planned 2026 purchasing. Brightwater trades diesel, jet fuel, and gasoline and operates storage terminals. Gas purchased at the Kestrel Hub supplies one blending site, so supplier pricing affects both trading margins and blending costs.

## Supplier Pricing Position

The internal view is that Ostrava Petrochem offers an average discount of USD 4.20 per tonne versus market. The figure is an internal estimate. It reflects the spread between Ostrava Petrochem's delivered pricing and the relevant market reference across the review period, and it should be treated as indicative rather than as a contractual guarantee.

Several points should be kept in mind when reading the discount:

- The average masks variation between product grades and delivery points.
- Freight, terminal handling, and timing differences can shift the effective margin on any single cargo.
- The discount is measured against market, so movements in the benchmark will move the realised advantage.

## Planned 2026 Volume Change

Brightwater plans to double its jet fuel volume with Ostrava Petrochem in 2026. The increase would concentrate more of our jet fuel sourcing with a single supplier, which raises both the value of the current discount and the exposure if supply or pricing terms change.

## Risks and Controls

Before the volume increase is committed, the team should:

- Confirm that the discount holds across the full range of jet fuel specifications we intend to buy.
- Review credit exposure and payment terms against the larger projected volume.
- Model the margin impact under adverse benchmark movements.
- Record all price and volume data in Corvane CTRM so that margin figures can be reconciled to trade records.

## Recommendation

Proceed with preparatory work on the expanded jet fuel volume, subject to the confirmation steps above. Revisit the discount estimate once the first quarter of 2026 settlements are available for comparison.
