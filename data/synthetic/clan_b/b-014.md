# Netting and payment procedure (wiki copy)

_Copied from the operations wiki; may be out of date._

**Company:** Brightwater Refined Products
**Document type:** Procedure
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01

## Purpose

This procedure describes how Brightwater Refined Products settles amounts owed between the company and its counterparties under its master agreements. It applies to diesel, jet fuel and gasoline transactions, including gas purchased at the Kestrel Hub to fuel the company's blending site. Settlement records are maintained in Corvane CTRM, the commodity trading platform the company uses, which is hosted as SaaS.

## Payment netting

Brightwater uses bilateral payment netting under its master agreements. Under this method, amounts payable in each direction between Brightwater and a counterparty are offset, and only the net balance is paid.

Netting applies only when both sides' payables exceed USD 250,000 in the month. If either side's payables for the month are at or below this threshold, netting does not apply and each invoice is settled separately under its own terms.

Settlements staff should confirm the monthly payables for both parties before deciding whether to net. Where the threshold is not met, the invoices are paid gross.

## Invoice disputes

Any invoice dispute must be raised within 10 business days of receipt. The dispute should state the invoice number, the disputed amount, and the reason for the objection, and should be recorded in Corvane CTRM against the relevant transaction.

Disputes received after the 10 business day period may not be accepted, so teams should log questions as soon as an invoice arrives. Undisputed portions of an invoice should be paid or netted on schedule.

## Responsibilities

- **Trading and operations teams** check invoice details against confirmed deals and flag discrepancies promptly.
- **Settlements** calculates monthly payables, applies the netting threshold, and records the outcome in Corvane CTRM.
- **Head of settlements** approves netting calculations and resolves escalated disputes.
