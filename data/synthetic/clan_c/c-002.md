# Provisional and Final Invoicing Procedure

## Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how Cerrado Agri Commodities issues provisional and final invoices for physical coffee, sugar, cocoa and soybean sales. It applies to all sales teams and to the settlements function, and it takes effect on 2025-10-01. Staff may quote this document when explaining invoicing timelines to customers.

## Invoicing sequence

The invoicing cycle has two stages.

1. **Provisional invoice.** A provisional invoice for 90 percent of contract value is issued on the bill of lading date. The sales desk confirms the bill of lading details in Harvest CTRM before the invoice is raised, and the provisional amount is calculated from the contracted price and the stated quantity.
2. **Final invoice.** The final invoice is issued after the weight and quality certificates arrive. Settlements checks the certificates against the contract specification, recalculates the value using the final weights and quality results, and credits the provisional amount. Any difference between the provisional and final value is shown as a separate adjustment line.

Final invoices are not issued until both certificates have been received and checked. If a certificate is missing or disputed, the file is held and the sales desk is informed the same working day.

## Payment terms

Payment terms are net 20 days from the bill of lading date. This applies to both the provisional and the final invoice, and the due date is printed on each invoice. Customers should be reminded of this term when the provisional invoice is sent.

## Currency

Invoices are issued in USD. The exception is Brazilian domestic sales, which are invoiced in BRL. Sales staff must confirm the invoicing currency at contract signature and must not change it on an existing contract without written approval from settlements.
