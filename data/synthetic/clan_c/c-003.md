# Harvest CTRM Settlement Configuration

## Purpose and scope

This document records how settlement is configured in Harvest CTRM, the commodity trading platform used by Cerrado Agri Commodities for coffee, sugar, cocoa, and soybean transactions. It applies to invoices raised against physical and priced contracts with European roasters and processors, as well as to supplier settlements for purchases sourced in Brazil and West Africa.

The configuration described here is effective from 2025-10-01. Staff may quote it when answering questions about how settlement works internally. Where this document and a signed contract differ, the contract governs.

## Settlement method

Cerrado uses **gross settlement** for all settlement cycles in Harvest CTRM. Each invoice is settled in full on its own terms. Payment netting is not enabled, so amounts owed by a counterparty are not offset against amounts Cerrado owes that same counterparty in the same cycle.

Operations and finance teams should therefore expect separate payment instructions for each receivable and each payable, even where the same counterparty appears on both sides of a period. Reconciliation should match each invoice to its own remittance.

## Invoicing schedule

Invoices are generated by the scheduled job **HV_INVOICE_DAILY**. The job runs every business day. Invoices created on a given day are queued for review by the contracts administration team before they are released to counterparties.

If a scheduled run fails, the contracts administration team should check the job log in Harvest CTRM and confirm whether the missed invoices need to be regenerated manually. Do not create duplicate invoices by hand without first confirming the job status.

## Invoice disputes

Counterparties must raise any invoice dispute within **15 calendar days** of receipt of the invoice. Calendar days include weekends and public holidays. Disputes received after that window may be referred to the commercial lead for a decision, but they are not automatically accepted.

Staff who receive a dispute should:

- Log it in Harvest CTRM against the relevant invoice number.
- Record the date of receipt of the original invoice so the deadline can be checked.
- Pass the dispute to the commercial lead for the contract concerned.

## Questions

Questions about this configuration should go to the finance systems owner for Harvest CTRM. Changes to settlement method or invoicing schedules require sign-off before they are applied to the live platform.
