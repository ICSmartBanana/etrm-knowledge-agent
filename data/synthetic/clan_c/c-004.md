## Email text

**Subject:** Settlement payment terms

Team,

For settlement of sales invoices, the payment terms in force at the time of this note are **net 30 days** from the invoice date. Please use this wording when you describe terms to European roasters and processors who ask about settlement timing.

Some points to keep in mind:

- The clock starts on the invoice date, not on the date of shipment or the date of delivery to the buyer's warehouse.
- Where a sale is recorded in Harvest CTRM, the invoice date shown in the platform is the reference date for the terms.
- If a buyer asks for different terms, do not confirm them in conversation. Refer the request to treasury so it can be reviewed against the contract.

This note was written before the 2024 contract template update. Any terms written into a signed contract take precedence over this email. Before quoting these terms in a new negotiation, check the current contract template to confirm the wording still matches.

Thanks,

Treasury team
Cerrado Agri Commodities
