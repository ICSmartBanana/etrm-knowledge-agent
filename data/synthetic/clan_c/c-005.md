# Coffee shipment logistics runbook

**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Owner:** Cerrado Agri Commodities Logistics
**Document type:** Runbook

## Purpose

This runbook describes how coffee shipments are prepared, documented and tracked from origin to delivery. It is written for logistics coordinators, trade desk staff and anyone who needs to quote the standard shipping steps to a customer or internal team.

## Scope

The procedure covers coffee lots loaded at Santos for delivery to European roasters and processors. Other commodities and origins follow separate runbooks.

## Container standard

Coffee is shipped from Santos in 20-foot containers. A standard container holds about 320 bags. Coordinators should confirm the bag count against the contract and the packing list before the container is sealed. Any variance above the contract tolerance must be reported to the trade desk the same day.

## Shipping instructions

Shipping instructions must be sent 10 days before the vessel's estimated arrival. This deadline gives the carrier and the terminal time to process the booking and the export documentation. Coordinators should check the vessel schedule weekly, because a revised estimated arrival changes the date on which the instructions are due.

If the instructions are late, the coordinator must notify the trade desk immediately and record the reason in the shipment file.

## Container tracking

The logistics module records each container under a CNT- reference. Staff should:

1. Create the CNT- record when the container is assigned to a lot.
2. Attach the packing list, the seal number and the weighbridge ticket.
3. Update the status at each milestone: gate-in at Santos, loaded on vessel, discharged, and delivered.

Do not reuse a CNT- reference for another container, even after the shipment is complete. Records are kept for audit purposes.

Harvest CTRM shows the current status of each CNT- record. If a status appears incorrect, check the physical container documents before making a correction.
