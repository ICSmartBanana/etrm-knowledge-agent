# Cocoa Position Management Procedure

**Company:** Cerrado Agri Commodities
**Document type:** Procedure
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01

## Purpose and scope

This procedure sets out how the cocoa book is managed at Cerrado Agri Commodities, from the point a physical cocoa purchase or sale is fixed through to the hedge being placed on futures and the position being monitored against limits. It applies to all cocoa positions held by the trading desk, including cargoes sourced in West Africa and sold to European roasters and processors. Staff should record all cocoa activity in Harvest CTRM, which is the commodity trading platform the desk uses for position keeping.

## Hedging of physical fixings

Cocoa positions are hedged on futures within two business days of physical fixing. The trader who fixes the physical price is responsible for confirming that a corresponding futures hedge is entered in Harvest CTRM within that window. Where a fixing is priced on a differential basis, the trader should record the differential separately so that the futures leg and the physical leg can be reconciled at month end.

If a hedge cannot be placed within the window, for example because of market closure or liquidity constraints, the trader must notify the desk head before the window closes and record the reason in the position comments field.

## Net open position limit

The cocoa net open position limit is 1,500 tonnes. The limit applies to the net of long and short exposure across physical and futures positions combined. Positions approaching the limit should be flagged to the risk function during the daily close process. No trader may take a position that would cause the net open position to exceed the limit without prior written approval.

## Record keeping

All fixings, hedges and limit checks must be retained in Harvest CTRM for the period required by internal policy. Manual adjustments to positions require a reason code and must be reviewed by a second member of the desk before end of day.
