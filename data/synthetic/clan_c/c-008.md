# Counterparty Sanctions Screening Policy (Version 1)

**Effective date:** 2022-02-01
**Document type:** Policy
**Applies to:** All Cerrado Agri Commodities staff involved in counterparty onboarding, trade capture, settlement, or credit management

## 1. Purpose

Cerrado Agri Commodities sources coffee, sugar, cocoa, and soybeans in Brazil and West Africa and sells to European roasters and processors. Because our trades pass through multiple jurisdictions, we must confirm that no counterparty, beneficial owner, or related party is subject to applicable sanctions. This policy sets out how we screen counterparties and how screening results are recorded.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers every legal entity that Cerrado Agri Commodities contracts with as a buyer, seller, broker, freight provider, warehouse operator, or agent. It applies to new and existing relationships alike.

## 3. Screening Requirements

3.1 **Onboarding screening.** Before any counterparty is approved in Harvest CTRM, the counterparty's legal name, registration number, registered address, and known beneficial owners must be screened against the sanctions lists that the compliance function has designated for the relevant jurisdictions.

3.2 **Periodic rescreening.** Counterparties are rescreened against sanctions lists every 12 months. Rescreening covers all counterparties with open or recently settled exposure, regardless of whether their details have changed.

3.3 **Event-driven rescreening.** A counterparty must also be rescreened when any of the following occurs: a change of ownership or control, a change of registered name or address, a request to redirect payments to a new bank or country, or publication of a relevant sanctions update that could affect the counterparty's jurisdiction.

## 4. Handling Potential Matches

4.1 A potential match must be escalated to the compliance function the same working day it is identified. The trader or operations user who found it must not approve, settle, or amend any open trade with that counterparty until compliance has cleared or rejected the match.

4.2 Compliance records the outcome of each review, including the list version consulted, the reviewer, and the reasoning for the decision. Records are retained with the counterparty file in Harvest CTRM.

4.3 A confirmed match results in a block on the counterparty record. Existing contracts are reviewed with legal counsel before any further action.

## 5. Roles

- **Compliance** owns the screening lists, performs match reviews, and maintains the screening log.
- **Operations** ensures that counterparty records are complete before submission for screening.
- **Traders** must not create trades with counterparties that are not in an approved status.

## 6. Record Keeping

Screening results, match reviews, and rescreening dates are retained in line with the company's standard record retention rules.

## 7. Questions

Staff with questions about this policy should raise them with the compliance function before proceeding with the transaction in question.
