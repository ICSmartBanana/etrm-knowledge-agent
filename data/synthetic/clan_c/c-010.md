# Grower relationship notes (internal)

**Internal - not for distribution**

**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Document type:** Analysis
**Prepared for:** Cerrado Agri Commodities, origination and risk teams

## Purpose

This note records the current internal view of our relationship with Serra Verde Growers, a cooperative supplying coffee to Cerrado Agri Commodities from our Brazilian sourcing network. It is intended to support decisions on pricing, contract terms, and credit exposure. It should be read alongside the latest contract summaries held in Harvest CTRM.

## Current assessment

Serra Verde Growers is a valuable supply partner, but its willingness to remain with Cerrado depends heavily on farm-gate economics. Our internal view is that the cooperative may switch buyers if prices fall below USD 3.10 per kg. Members who face weaker returns have more options to sell to other exporters, and the cooperative's board has signalled that it will review buyer terms if market conditions deteriorate.

Because of this sensitivity, the price floor and payment timing should be treated as the main levers in retaining the relationship. Pricing discussions should be framed around the cooperative's margin position rather than only around our European customer commitments.

## Financing considerations

Cerrado is considering a pre-financing facility of USD 5 million for Serra Verde Growers. The facility would help members cover input costs and harvest labour before delivery, which should strengthen loyalty and secure volume for the next season. It would also increase our credit exposure to a single counterparty, so any approval should be conditional on:

- Delivery commitments recorded against the facility in Harvest CTRM
- Clear repayment mechanics linked to shipment settlement
- Regular review of the cooperative's cash position and member deliveries
- Confirmation from the credit committee that the exposure fits current limits

## Recommended next steps

1. Model margin scenarios for the cooperative at prices around and below USD 3.10 per kg.
2. Draft a term sheet for the pre-financing facility, with the amount stated as a pre-financing facility of USD 5 million, subject to credit approval.
3. Review the cooperative's delivery history and identify members with the highest default or side-selling risk.
4. Schedule a relationship review before the next contract renewal.
