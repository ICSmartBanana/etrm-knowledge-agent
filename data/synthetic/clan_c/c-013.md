# Harvest CTRM month-end close runbook (wiki copy)

_Copied from the operations wiki; may be out of date._

**Company:** Cerrado Agri Commodities
**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Document type:** Runbook

## Purpose

This runbook describes the standard steps the finance and operations teams follow to close the books for each month in Harvest CTRM, the commodity trading platform used across Cerrado Agri Commodities' coffee, sugar, cocoa, and soybean books. It applies to all trade capture, valuation, and reconciliation activity that feeds the monthly ledger. Staff may quote this document when explaining close timing or responsibilities.

## Close Timeline

The month-end close starts on business day 2 of the following month. Trade data for the closing period should be frozen in Harvest CTRM before the close begins, so that late-captured trades are either booked to the correct period or flagged for review.

Close must be signed off by the controller by business day 5. Any open items that cannot be cleared by business day 5 should be escalated to the controller in writing, with a short description of the item, its estimated value, and the proposed resolution date.

## Steps

1. **Freeze the period.** Confirm that all physical and paper trades dated in the closing month are captured and validated. Reject or hold entries that lack confirmed counterparties or pricing.
2. **Reconcile positions.** Compare open contract quantities in Harvest CTRM against warehouse receipts, bills of lading, and third-party confirmations for each commodity.
3. **Revalue unrealised P&L.** Run the scheduled revaluation job HV_MTM_MONTHEND against the approved end-of-month price curves. Review the job log for failed or skipped contracts before proceeding.
4. **Review exceptions.** Investigate any contract whose mark-to-market movement falls outside the normal tolerance band, and document the cause.
5. **Post journals.** Generate the realised and unrealised P&L journals and post them to the general ledger once the revaluation results are reviewed.
6. **Obtain sign-off.** Circulate the close pack, including the reconciliation summary and exception log, to the controller for approval.

## Roles and Escalation

Operations staff own steps one through three. Finance owns steps four and five. The controller holds final approval authority for the close. If a step cannot be completed on schedule, the owner should notify the controller at once rather than waiting for the deadline.
