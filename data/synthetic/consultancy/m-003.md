# Gas Scheduling Concepts (Vendor-Neutral)

**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Document type:** Guide
**Owner:** Meridian ETRM Consulting Knowledge Base

## Purpose

This guide gives consultants a vendor-neutral overview of how gas scheduling works in an ETRM or CTRM context. It is a generic playbook. It contains no client-specific facts, and staff may quote it in discussions with clients. Any specific rule must be confirmed against the client's own documented procedure before it is applied.

## Core Concepts

**Gas day.** A gas day is the operating period over which gas flows are scheduled and measured. It usually starts at a fixed local time, but that time varies by market and by hub. Consultants should never assume a start time from another market. Confirm the gas day definition with the client during discovery and record it in the configuration notes.

**Nominations.** A nomination is the request to move a defined quantity of gas at a point on a given gas day. Nomination deadlines, the units used, and renomination rules differ by hub and by contract. Some contracts allow intraday renominations, while others do not. Treat every timing and unit rule as client-specific until it has been verified.

**Confirmations.** A confirmation is the agreed record that the counterparties and the scheduling parties accept a nominated quantity. Scheduling is not complete until the expected confirmations have been received or the exception has been logged.

**Batch and manual activity.** Most scheduling flows run as a batch process, followed by a window for review. Manual activity sits on top of that batch and is where many problems start.

## Common Failure Points

1. **Manual edits after the batch run.** When a user changes a nomination or volume after the batch has completed, the platform state may no longer match the upstream records or the counterparty view. Always check whether a post-batch edit has been reflected everywhere it needs to be, and document who made the change and why.
2. **Missing confirmations.** A nomination without a matching confirmation creates an open item that can surface later as a imbalance or a dispute. Build a daily check that lists nominations lacking confirmations and route them for follow-up.
3. **Assumed rules.** Applying timing or unit rules from one hub or contract to another is a frequent source of error. Verify each rule against the governing documentation.

## Consultant Checklist

- Confirm the gas day start time for each market and hub in scope.
- Obtain the client's nomination deadlines, units, and renomination rules for each contract.
- Set up exception reports for post-batch manual edits and unconfirmed nominations.
- Record all verified rules in the client's configuration notes, not in this generic guide.

## Related Use

Use this guide to frame discussions and design questions. For any figure, deadline, or unit that will affect live scheduling, follow the client's own procedure and escalate to the client's operations contact if the source is unclear.
