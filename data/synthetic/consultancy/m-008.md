# ETRM Glossary

**Meridian ETRM Consulting**
Effective date: 2025-10-01

This glossary defines common terms used across energy trading and risk management (ETRM) and commodity trading and risk management (CTRM) engagements. Staff may quote these definitions in client correspondence, project documents, and training material. Where a client's contract or market rules differ from a general definition, the contract or rules govern.

## Scheduling and Physical Delivery Terms

**Gas day**
The defined period over which gas flows are measured and contracted. A gas day may not coincide with a calendar day, so teams should confirm the start point the relevant market uses before booking volumes.

**Nomination**
A request, submitted by a shipper or trader to a pipeline or transport operator, specifying the quantity of gas or product to be moved on a given day at a given point.

**Renomination**
A revised nomination submitted after the original nomination, usually to adjust volumes within the same gas day or to reflect changed market conditions. Renomination windows and limits are set by the transport operator.

**Gate closure**
The deadline after which nominations or changes to nominations can no longer be submitted for a given period. Missing gate closure generally means the change will not be scheduled until the next opportunity.

**Bill of lading**
A document issued by a carrier that acknowledges receipt of cargo, sets out its quantity and condition, and evidences the terms of carriage. In ETRM systems it is often used as the trigger for title transfer and invoicing.

**Laytime**
The period agreed in a charter party during which the charterer may load or discharge a vessel without additional payment. Laytime is typically counted in hours or days and begins or ends according to the terms of the charter.

**Demurrage**
The payment owed by the charterer to the shipowner when a vessel is held beyond the agreed laytime. Demurrage is the counterpart of despatch, where a vessel is discharged or loaded early and the owner pays the charterer.

## Valuation and Settlement Terms

**Mark-to-market (MTM)**
The valuation of open positions at current market prices, with the resulting gain or loss recognised in the period. MTM figures are used for risk reporting, margining, and, depending on accounting policy, financial statements.

**Netting**
The offsetting of amounts owed between two parties, or of positions with the same counterparty, so that a single net amount is settled. Netting reduces settlement volume and credit exposure. The legal enforceability of netting depends on the governing master agreement.

## Using This Glossary

If a term in a client's contract conflicts with the definitions above, the contract prevails. Consultants should record any client-specific variation in the project documentation rather than changing this glossary. This document contains only generic definitions and does not hold client-specific information.
